Machiwari 町割

In Edo, machiwari was the dividing of the town into streets, blocks and plots: the plan everything else was built on. Machiwari does that for an enterprise's architecture.

In development

One model, every view

An architect models the enterprise once: its capabilities, processes, applications, data, technology and the decisions that shape them. Diagrams, registers, roadmaps and a complete TOGAF dossier are drawn from that one model, so they never drift apart.

Machiwari is a commercial product from Innovenso. Its foundation, the architecture model and the language to describe it, is part of the open Taihei Framework.

Interested?

Machiwari is used in Innovenso's own architecture work today and is not yet generally available. To hear when it is, or to talk about your architecture practice, write to hello@taihei.dev.
